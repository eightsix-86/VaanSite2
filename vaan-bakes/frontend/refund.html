Refund Policy

Your satisfaction is our priority

🛡️

Our Commitment to Quality

At Vaan Bakes, we are committed to delivering the highest quality baked goods to our customers. Every product is crafted with love, care, and the finest ingredients. However, we understand that sometimes things may not go as planned, and we're here to make it right.

✅

Refund Eligibility

You may be eligible for a refund or replacement under the following circumstances:

  • Damaged Products: If your order arrives damaged or in unsatisfactory condition
  • Wrong Order: If you receive an incorrect product or flavor
  • Quality Issues: If the product does not meet our quality standards
  • Late Delivery: If delivery is significantly delayed beyond the promised time
  • Missing Items: If items are missing from your order
⏰

Refund Request Time Frame

Refund requests must be submitted within 24 hours of delivery. Please contact us immediately if you encounter any issues with your order. Late requests may not be eligible for refunds.

📧

How to Request a Refund

  1. Contact us via email at hello@vaanbakes.com or call +1 (555) 123-4567
  2. Provide your order number and details about the issue
  3. Include clear photos of the product if applicable
  4. Our team will review your request within 24 hours
  5. Once approved, refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days
❌

Non-Refundable Items

The following items are not eligible for refunds:

  • Products that have been partially consumed
  • Custom or personalized orders (unless defective)
  • Orders placed more than 24 hours ago without prior contact
  • Products damaged due to improper storage after delivery
  • Change of mind or taste preference
🔄

Replacement Option

In many cases, we prefer to offer a replacement product rather than a refund. This ensures you still get to enjoy our delicious baked goods! Replacements will be delivered at no additional cost within 24-48 hours of approval.

💳

Refund Processing

Once your refund request is approved, the refund will be processed to your original payment method. Please allow:

  • Credit/Debit Cards: 5-7 business days
  • Online Payment Platforms: 3-5 business days
  • Bank Transfers: 7-10 business days
🚫

Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your order for a full refund if:

  • The order has not yet been prepared (within 2 hours of placing the order)
  • The order has not been dispatched for delivery
  • You contact us before the preparation begins

Note: Orders that are already in preparation or have been dispatched cannot be cancelled but may be eligible for a refund upon delivery if you refuse to accept it.

📞
